Hello, I'm Kenichi — a blue cat living in Taiwan who enjoys coding and photography.

This is where I organize my work and life snippets, mainly artwork I commissioned and photos. Rather than posting on social platforms, I'd prefer to make this a more complete and readable space.

If you're here for the first time, you can start with [Art Collection](/gallery) and [Photos](/photos).<br>
[Art Collection](/gallery) focuses on character-related commission work, while [Photos](/photos) is where I collect the photos I take day to day — mostly furries and scenery.

I also enjoy building small things, which you can find on my GitHub.

This blog may be used later for short posts — like project updates, site changes, or whatever I've been up to lately.

Thanks for stopping by. Hope you have a good time here.
